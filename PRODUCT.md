## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS, following the PRD recommendation. Deploy target undecided.

## Users

- Developers use PlayTest ID to configure Android app tests, recruit testers, monitor progress, and review feedback, bugs, and reports.
- Testers discover available Android apps, join tests, complete validated missions, submit feedback or bugs, and redeem points for rewards.
- Admins and super admins operate the platform by managing users, apps and tests, transactions, support reports, rewards, and platform settings.

## Product Purpose

PlayTest ID provides an end-to-end platform for structured human testing of Android apps before release or update. It helps developers run and monitor tests, testers complete clear testing missions and earn validated rewards, and admins oversee platform operations. Product success includes completed test cycles, activated testers, completed missions, valid bug reports, successful payouts, repeat developer use, and timely support resolution.

## Positioning

PlayTest ID connects Android developers with tester cohorts and coordinates the full testing cycle through configurable eligibility and quotas, daily missions, validated activity, feedback and bug reporting, progress monitoring, and reward tracking.

## Operating Context

The product has three connected role areas: Developer, Tester, and Admin. Developers and admins are desktop-first; the tester experience is mobile-first, with important components remaining responsive. A typical cycle runs from APK upload and test configuration through tester recruitment, missions, validation, feedback and bug reporting, rewards, and reporting. The MVP is focused on Android app testing in Indonesia; cross-border payments are out of scope except for payment methods configured by the platform.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The PRD defines authentication and role-based access, developer profiles and test creation, APK upload and validation, tester discovery and enrollment, configurable missions and quotas, feedback and bug reports, progress monitoring, points and reward redemption, admin operations, histories, exports, and audit trails. MVP priorities include the core test cycle and basic admin operations; payment gateway integration, payout, notifications, history, exports, advanced filtering, and ticketing are prioritized later. iOS distribution, social networking, app trading, and automated UI testing as a replacement for human testers are out of scope for MVP. Prices, fees, quotas, durations, point conversion, and redemption minimums must remain configurable; figures shown in the PRD's source designs are mock data, not final business values. APKs, personal data, and test evidence require role-based access and appropriate security controls. Accessibility is a project requirement; a specific conformance standard remains undecided.

## Brand Commitments

The user requests a modern, clean, professional, responsive experience for desktop, tablet, and mobile, with clear visual hierarchy, readable typography, consistent spacing, reusable components, accessibility, and a distinctive, non-generic UI.

## Product Principles

- Make each role's next action and current status clear.
- Keep test eligibility, quotas, missions, and reward rules configurable.
- Tie rewards to validated testing activity.
- Keep transactions, status changes, and operational decisions traceable.
- Protect APKs, personal information, and testing evidence by role.
